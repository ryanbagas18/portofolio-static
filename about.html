About Me

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computing (S.Kom) in Information Systems from Universitas Surabaya (UBAYA), where I developed a strong foundation in full-stack web development, systems analysis, and business process design. My technical work revolves around building scalable web applications using Laravel, managing MySQL databases, and prototyping user interfaces in Figma.

Beyond writing code, I actively hone my leadership, team management, and operational troubleshooting skills. As a Vocal Trainer for UBAYA Choir, I directed talent development and technical preparations that led the team to secure 4 Gold Medals in national and international competitions. Additionally, my role as Steering Committee and Head of Logistics for FESPA UBAYA strengthened my cross-functional communication and risk mitigation skills in handling large-scale events.

For my final capstone project, I translated these two domains into a practical digital solution by developing a web-based Recruitment and Training Management Information System to digitize membership workflows for UBAYA Choir. I am driven by taking complex organizational processes and turning them into simple, elegant digital solutions.
